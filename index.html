ABOUT US

Big questions can begin with small voices.

Whispers of Wonders is built around children's curiosity. Young contributors write, draw, investigate, invent puzzles and ask experts the questions they genuinely want answered. Adults help with research, editing and phrasing—but the curiosity belongs to the children.

OUR NEXT ADVENTURE · NOVEMBER 2026

History &
Heritage

Every family has a story. Every community carries memories, traditions and experiences that help shape who we are.

This November, our young contributors will explore their own histories and cultural heritage: family stories, celebrations, food, music, languages, crafts and memories shared across generations.

What traditions make your family special? What stories do your parents or grandparents remember? Join us in celebrating the cultures, memories and personal histories that make our world wonderfully diverse.
